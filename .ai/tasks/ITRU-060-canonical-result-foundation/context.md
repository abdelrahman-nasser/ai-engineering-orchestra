ledger plus permanent Dispatch-wide Entry. It includes exact descriptive Entry,
complete Run/Tool binding and captured ledger provenance. Three terminal outcomes
separate proven pre-content failure from possible/partial content effect.

raw Entry values, adapter-returned lookalikes, copied mappings and serialization

persistence nor a schema. The private optional Result return extension preserves
Entry/invocation diagnostics, while the public Admission result union is intact.
